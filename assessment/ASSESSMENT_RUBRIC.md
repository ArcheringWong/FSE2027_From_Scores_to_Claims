# Independent assessment rubric for the RQ2 contrasts

Two additional human evaluators independently assessed all 12 proposed contrasts. They were not involved in mapping generation or adjudication and did not have access to the analysts' forms. Their responses were frozen before reconciliation.

For each case, the evaluators considered the Table 3 scientific claim together with the source-grounded paraphrase and quotation, the reported endpoint and validation, the proposed alternative, and the proposed discriminating observation.

| Field | Assessment question |
|---|---|
| `q1_claim_fidelity` | Does the Table 3 scientific claim faithfully represent the interpretation supported by the cited source material? |
| `q2_alternative_compatibility` | Given the reported endpoint and existing validation, could the proposed alternative still plausibly account for the reported result? |
| `q3_predictive_discrimination` | Would the proposed observation make the Table 3 claim and alternative predict meaningfully different outcomes? |
| `q4_construct_alignment` | Does the observation target the distinguishing scientific property beyond merely adding another endpoint measurement? |
| `q5_design_isolation` | As written, does the comparison isolate the intended contrast sufficiently for interpretation? |

Allowed categorical answers were `YES`, `UNCERTAIN`, and `NO`. Allowed overall dispositions were `RETAIN AS WRITTEN`, `RETAIN WITH MINOR CLARIFICATION`, `REVISE CONTRAST`, and `REJECT CONTRAST`. The evaluators were asked to state expected results under both accounts using the same unit, comparison, and measurement and to explain source or design concerns in `brief_rationale`.
